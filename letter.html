My dearest Bachhaaa ♡

A little piece of my heart

Pata hai baabeee, kabhi-kabhi main sochta hoon ki kuchu puchhuu tumse...

“Tum itni pyaari kaise ho?”

Sach bolu toh mujhe khud nahi pata ki kab tum itni important ban gayi. Tumhari hasi, tumhara gussa, tumhari cuteness aur tumhara woh random sa bakbak karna — sab kuch dheere-dheere mere liye bahut special ho gaya.

Mujhe tumhari perfect version nahi chahiye. Mujhe tum chahiye.

Haan, tum mujhe irritate karti ho, chidhati ho, kabhi-kabhi pareshan bhi karti ho... lekin pata nahi kyun, in sab cheezon ke bina bhi kuch adhura sa lagta hai.

Agar mujhe ek baar phir chance mile tumhe choose karne ka, toh shayad main phir se tumhe hi choose karunga.

Bas ek cheez hamesha same rehni chahiye — humare beech ki woh stupid si hasi, random conversations, chhoti-chhoti fights, manana, care aur “baabeee” wali feeling. Main nahi chahta ki yeh kabhi purani ho.

Tum meri favourite notification ho... favourite conversation ho... aur honestly, meri favourite problem bhi. ♡

Kuchu puchhu?

Bas itna yaad rakhna, Bachhaaa— Tum bahut special ho. Bahut zyada.

With all my love,

Prakhar
